Human Body Atlas

A complete, interactive model of the adult male and female human body with over 3,700 individually selectable structures: every bone and tooth, muscles and tendons, joints and ligaments, the heart, arteries and veins, brain regions, spinal tracts, cranial and spinal nerves, sense organs, viscera, endocrine glands, lymph nodes and skin regions.

Touch

  • Rotate one finger · Zoom / pan two fingers
  • Tap a structure to select it · Double-tap to zoom to it
  • Drag the panel at the bottom up for layers, the anatomy tree and details

Mouse & keyboard

  • Rotate left-drag · Pan right-drag · Zoom scroll · Shift-click add to selection
  • H hide · I isolate · A show all · F focus · G fade others · Esc clear
  • 1 3D · 2 X-ray · 3 slice · R reset camera · / search · ↑/↓ move slice

Animations

The pulse button beside the model animates the heartbeat with blood pulsing out through the arteries and returning through the veins, nerve impulses spreading from the brain and spinal cord, and food moving through the digestive tract. Selecting a heart, vessel, nerve or part of the gut also offers Animate.
