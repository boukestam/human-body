Human Body Atlas

A complete, interactive model of the adult human body with over 3,600 individually selectable structures: every bone and tooth, muscles and tendons, joints and ligaments, the heart, arteries and veins, brain regions, spinal tracts, cranial and spinal nerves, sense organs, viscera, endocrine glands, lymph nodes and skin regions.

Controls

  • Rotate left-drag · Pan right-drag / shift-drag · Zoom scroll / pinch
  • Click a structure to select it · Shift-click to add to selection · Double-click to focus
  • H hide selection · I isolate · A show all · F focus · G fade others · Esc clear
  • 1 3D view · 2 X-ray · 3 slice · R reset camera
  • In slice mode use ↑/↓ or the slider to move the cutting plane; the panel shows the exact 2D cross-section (atlas, CT or MRI style).
